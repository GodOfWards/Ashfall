# The Argentine calendar around February 2025

Real-world facts, not canon until a decision adopts them (`../Ashfall_Canon.md`
records what's decided). See `README.md` for the status labels. Gathered for
the lore session on how the collapse played out in Lima (#329): who was in
town, and who was away, in the weeks around the collapse.

## Summer holidays

- **Families take their holidays in two-week blocks (*quincenas*)**, and
  January and February are the peak. A two-week holiday typically falls
  somewhere between **the second half of January and the first half of
  February** (Tom, from living there, 2026-09-29). Status: Tom's first-hand
  knowledge.
- **Tourism reporting for summer 2025 is framed by quincena.** The national
  Secretaría de Turismo projected more than 3.9 million tourists at national
  destinations during January 2025. The Atlantic coast (Mar del Plata, Villa
  Gesell, Pinamar) is the most chosen destination. Destinations in southern
  Neuquén reported up to 90 % booked for the first half of February.
  Secondary (search summaries, 2026-09-29):
  [Argentina.gob.ar, "Temporada 2025"](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/temporada-2025-gran-movimiento-turistico-en-todo-el-pais),
  [Ámbito](https://www.ambito.com/informacion-general/verano-2025-los-destinos-mas-visitados-todo-el-pais-la-primera-quincena-del-ano-n6104342).
- **Why two weeks: the labour law's minimum.** The Ley de Contrato de Trabajo
  (Ley 20.744) gives **14 consecutive days** of paid leave a year up to five
  years' seniority; 21 days over five, 28 over ten, 35 over twenty (art. 150).
  The employer must grant it **between 1 October and 30 April**, notified in
  writing at least 45 days ahead (art. 154). Secondary, 2026-09-29:
  [Wikisource](https://es.m.wikisource.org/wiki/Ley_de_Contrato_de_Trabajo_(Argentina)/T%C3%ADtulo_V_vacaciones_y_otras_licencias),
  [leyes-ar.com, art. 154](https://leyes-ar.com/ley_de_contrato_de_trabajo/154.htm).
  What would confirm it: the law's text on InfoLEG.

## School

- **Buenos Aires province's 2025 school year began on Wednesday 5 March
  2025**, for every level (inicial, primario, secundario and the rest). It
  ended on 22 December 2025, with the winter break from 21 July to
  1 August. Secondary, 2026-09-29:
  [La Nación, 2025-01-30](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/sociedad/calendario-escolar-pba-cuando-empiezan-las-clases-2025-en-la-provincia-de-buenos-aires-nid30012025/).
  Infobae gave **Monday 3 March** on 2024-12-17
  ([Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/sociedad/2024/12/17/calendario-escolar-cuando-empiezan-las-clases-en-provincia-de-buenos-aires-en-2025/));
  the later date stands. That 3 and 4 March were the Carnival holidays
  (below) is the likely reason for the change; no source says so. What
  would confirm it: the province's own calendar (abc.gob.ar, "Calendario
  Escolar 2025", which returned 503 when tried).
- **So children were out of school for all of February 2025.** When the 2024
  school year ended in the province is **unconfirmed** (the sources read
  don't say).

## Holidays

- **Carnival 2025 was Monday 3 and Tuesday 4 March**, national non-movable
  holidays: the year's first extra-long weekend. Carnival follows Easter,
  which fell in April in 2025. Secondary, 2026-09-29:
  [Infobae, 2025-03-02](https://www.infobae.com/sociedad/2025/03/02/carnaval-2025-por-que-son-feriados-el-3-y-4-de-marzo/),
  [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/feriados/2025/cuando-es-el-feriado-de-carnaval-2025-en-la-argentina-nid19022025/).
  So Carnival did **not** fall in February 2025. Whether February had any
  other national holiday is unconfirmed.
