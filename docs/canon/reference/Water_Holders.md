# Water holders — what a thermos, a bucket, a canteen and a water jug hold and weigh

Real-world facts, not canon until a decision adopts them (`../Ashfall_Canon.md`
records what's decided). See `README.md` for the status labels. Gathered
2026-10-01 for #393: #47 needs the mate thermos's capacity, and #394 needs
the bucket's, the canteen's and the water jug's capacities and empty weights.
Brands are named here as sources only; none appears in the game.

Most figures come from retail listings, not makers' spec sheets, so they are
**Secondary** unless marked otherwise. Where several listings agree, the
range is given.

## Mate thermos (*termo*)

- **Sizes sold for mate: 500 mL, 750 mL, 1 L and 1.2 L.** 1 L is the most
  common listing across Argentine retailers; 1.2 L is the size of the
  best-selling premium stainless model. Secondary, 2026-10-01:
  [Mi Matero, 1 L thermoses](https://www.mimatero.com.ar/termos1/termos-de-1-lt/),
  [Mi Matero, 750 mL](https://www.mimatero.com.ar/productos/termo-750-ml/).
- **Stainless steel, 1 L, empty: 606–750 g.** Four listings: 606 g (AFT
  Grupo), 643 g (Electrolux), 704 g (Driven), 750 g (Mundo Set Matero).
  Secondary, 2026-10-01:
  [AFT Grupo](https://www.aftgrupo.com/productos/ref-05075015),
  [Electrolux](https://www.electrolux.cl/240093825-botella-termica-1000ml-negra-electrolux/p),
  [Driven](https://www.driven.com.ar/termicos/82-termo-de-acero-inoxidable-1-litro-negro-7798350916234.html),
  [Mundo Set Matero](https://www.mundosetmatero.com/producto/termo-acero-inoxidable-1-litro-personalizado/).
- **Stainless steel, 1.2 L, empty: 912 g** (Stanley Mate System). Secondary,
  2026-10-01:
  [Megatone listing](https://www.megatone.net/producto/mate-system-stanley-classic-12-lts-maple_MKT0334WES/).
- **Plastic body with a glass liner (*ampolla*), 1 L, empty: 530–545 g.**
  530 g (Mor Diva), 545 g (Tramontina Exata, from a search summary; the
  maker's page did not show its spec table). Secondary, 2026-10-01:
  [Mor Diva listing](https://www.paratucasa.com.ar/MLA-914370912-termo-ampolla-de-vidrio-diva-flip-1-litro-pico-vertedor-mor-_JM),
  [Tramontina Exata](https://www.tramontina.com.ar/termo-exata-tramontina-de-plastico-rojo-ampolla-de-vidrio-tapa-rosca-1l-61637506/p).
- **The game's figure disagrees.** `thermos` weighs 0.4 kg, lighter than
  every 1 L thermos found. Recorded on #47; not fixed here.

## Household bucket (*balde*)

- **Common size: 10 L,** the standard household listing. Secondary,
  2026-10-01:
  [Jumbo](https://www.jumbo.com.ar/balde-deses-plast-10l/p),
  [Papelera Rivadavia](https://www.papelerarivadavia.com.ar/producto/balde-plastico-10-lts/),
  [Battaglia](https://www.battagliasrl.com.ar/inicio/29-balde-plastico-10-litros.html),
  [Florek](http://florek.com.ar/10litros.html).
- **Empty: 385–500 g,** HDPE or polypropylene. One listing gives 385 g for
  the body and 158 g for a lid; another gives 500 g. Secondary, 2026-10-01,
  from a search summary of the listings above.

## Canteen (*cantimplora*)

- **Common size: 1 L.**
- **What Argentine shops sell: mostly the military-style plastic canteen**,
  1 L, with an aluminium cup that nests under it and a cloth cover. It is the
  type most listed on Mercado Libre Argentina; aluminium bottles are sold too.
  One Argentine shop gives **0.4 kg** for the canteen with its cup (and
  presumably cover). Secondary, 2026-10-01:
  [Maverick Indumentaria](https://maverickindumentaria.mitiendanube.com/productos/cantimplora-tactica-militar-con-jarro-de-aluminio/),
  [Mercado Libre Argentina, military canteens](https://listado.mercadolibre.com.ar/cantimploras-militares).
  A Chilean listing of the same type gives 348 g with cup and cover
  ([Ekol](https://www.ekol.cl/cantimplora-verde-militar-plastica-copy)).
- **Aluminium bottle, 1 L, empty: 130–200 g** bare: 130 g (Highlander),
  136 g (Laken), 174 g with cap (Quechua MH500: body 127 g, cap 47 g),
  200 g (Hosa, with carabiner). 338 g with a cooking cup and felt cover
  (Laken). European listings, not Argentine. Secondary, 2026-10-01:
  [Peregrinoteca](https://www.peregrinoteca.com/cantimplora-aluminio-highlander-1-litro-azul.html),
  [Decathlon](https://www.decathlon.es/es/p/cantimplora-de-montana-y-trekking-apertura-rapida-1l-aluminio-quechua-mh500/_/R-p-330041),
  [Camping Sport](https://campingsport.es/botellas-cantimploras/1319-botella-cantimplora-hosa-aluminio-mosqueton-1-litro-azul.html),
  [Laken](https://www.laken.es/en/products/aluminium-canteen-cooking-cup).

## Water jug (*bidón*)

Two kinds, and households meet both.

### Returnable dispenser jugs: 20 L and 12 L

- **Delivered full to homes and offices for a dispenser, in 20 L and 12 L;**
  the empty jug is collected on the next delivery. Polycarbonate or PET,
  with a handle. A 12 L jug is about 33 cm tall × 25 cm across; a 20 L,
  about 48 × 25 cm. Confirmed, 2026-10-01, on Argentine makers' and
  bottlers' pages:
  [Aro Plastyc (Córdoba), 12 L PC](https://www.aroplastyc.com/producto.php?id=208),
  [Aro Plastyc, 20 L PC](http://aroplastyc.com/producto.php?id=259),
  [Aro Plastyc, 20 L PET](https://www.aroplastyc.com/producto.php?id=539),
  [Wasserkraft (Burzaco), 12 L PC](https://wasserkraft.com.ar/producto/bidon-policarbonato-12-litros-con-manija/),
  [BidonesPET (Rosario): 20 L and 12 L returnable](https://bidonespet.com.ar/),
  [Aqualem: 20 and 12 L returnable PC](https://www.aqualem.com.ar/portfolio_page/bidones/).
  Dimensions and the delivery cycle: Secondary,
  [Somos el Agua](https://www.somoselagua.com.ar/productos/botellones-de-agua-retornables-ivess/),
  [Dodaro](https://dodaro.com.ar/agua-en-bidones/).
- **Empty, 20 L: 750–820 g.** PET: 750 g tare (two Chilean listings).
  Polycarbonate, 18.9 L (5 US gal): 820 g on a maker's page; another maker
  gives 750–780 g depending on moulding. No Argentine maker publishes a
  weight. Secondary, 2026-10-01:
  [Importadora Dali (PET)](https://importadoradali.cl/producto/botellon-pet-retornable-20-lts-azul-750gr/),
  [Quimey Ko (PET)](https://quimeyko.cl/aguapurificada/bidon-pet-de-20-litros-vacio.html),
  [Drink-Pack (PC)](https://www.drink-pack.com/product/water-bottles/polycarbonate-5-gallon-water-bottle-with-handle.html).
- **Empty, 12 L: Unconfirmed.** No maker or listing found states it. An
  estimate scaled from the 20 L figures is **about 0.5–0.6 kg**; a maker's
  spec sheet would confirm it.

### Supermarket single-use jugs: 6–6.5 L

- **The size a household keeps in the pantry as drinking water: 6.25 L**,
  still mineral water in a PET jug with a handle, stocked by Carrefour, DIA
  and Más Online under several labels; 6 L and 6.5 L are sold too. Secondary,
  2026-10-01:
  [Carrefour](https://supermercado.carrefour.com.ar/agua-mineral-sin-gas-villa-del-sur-bidon-625-l/p),
  [Más Online](https://www.masonline.com.ar/bidon-agua-mineral-check-sin-gas-6-25-l-2/p),
  [DIA](https://diaonline.supermercadosdia.com.ar/bebidas/aguas/aguas-sin-gas).
- **Empty, 6 L: 87–94 g** of PET, without handle or cap. The maker also
  makes 5, 8, 9 and 10 L single-use jugs. Confirmed, 2026-10-01:
  [Envapet, 6 L rectangular jug](https://www.envapet.com.ar/bidon-descartable-pet-06l-rectangular/)
  (an Argentine maker). With handle and cap, about 0.1 kg (Unconfirmed: the
  page gives no weight for them).
