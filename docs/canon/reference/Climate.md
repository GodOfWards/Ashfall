## Outdoor temperatures: the nearest official normals

**Lima has no SMN station.** The nearest is **San Fernando Aero** (34.455 S,
58.584 W, 3 m), about 70 km southeast and, like Lima, beside the Paraná
delta. The nearest inland is **Pergamino INTA** (33.93 S, 60.55 W, 65 m),
about 125 km west. Distances derived from the coordinates.

**San Fernando Aero, normals 1991–2020** (its record runs 1996–2020), in °C.
**Primary**, read 2026-10-03: Servicio Meteorológico Nacional,
*Estadísticas climatológicas normales 1991–2020*, ISSN 2953-5549
([PDF](https://repositorio.smn.gob.ar/bitstream/handle/20.500.12160/2506/estad%C3%ADsticas_climatol%C3%B3gicas_normales_1991-2020.pdf?sequence=4&isAllowed=y)).

- **February, the game's month:** a mean of 23.5 °C, days around 28 °C,
  nights around 19 °C; the hottest February day on record was 36.8 °C
  (2007).
- **Pergamino INTA, inland, runs about 1 °C cooler:** a mean of 23.3 °C in
  January, 22.1 °C in February, 9.1 °C in July and 16.6 °C for the year
  (same source, 1991–2019).
- **Lima's own figures are unconfirmed.** It lies between the two stations,
  nearer the river; San Fernando's are the better stand-in.

## Indoor temperature in an unheated house

- **No measurement for a Buenos Aires province house was found.** The
  closest is from Oberá, Misiones, a warmer and wetter climate:
  - an unheated social-housing house of hollow ceramic brick, 18 cm, with a
    sheet-metal roof, measured for a week in winter (26 July to 1 August);
  - **the indoor daily minimum averaged 12.2 °C against 5.9 °C outside**;
  - on one day, **an outdoor swing of 12.5 °C gave 8.78 °C indoors**: "un
    escaso efecto amortiguador de la masa del edificio";
  - **the daily maximums indoors and out stayed close together.**

  **Primary** for that house, read 2026-10-03: C. R. Brázzola and J. D.
  Czajkowski, "Evaluación higrotérmica en viviendas económicas.
  Comportamiento de invierno de la 'casa cajón'. Estudio de caso", ASADES
  ([PDF](http://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/93794/Documento_completo.pdf?sequence=1)).
  The paper's year wasn't read.
- **For Lima, derived and unconfirmed:** an unheated brick house follows the
  outdoor air, damped. Its daily maximum is near the outdoor one, and its
  nights stay several degrees warmer than outside. In February, that means
  **roughly 22–28 °C indoors**, from San Fernando's means. The game can use
  the outdoor monthly mean as the room's temperature until #58.
- **What would confirm it:** a thermometer in a Lima row house, or a
  hygrothermal monitoring study from the Buenos Aires region (the AVERMA
  journal publishes several; none for Zárate turned up).
